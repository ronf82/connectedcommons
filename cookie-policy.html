1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as local storage, which this policy also refers to as "cookies". They help a site remember your choices and understand how it is used.

2. How we use them

When you first visit connectedcommons.co we show a banner. You can accept all, reject all non-essential cookies, or choose by category. Until you choose, only essential cookies are used and no optional scripts load. Your choice is stored on your device for [12] months, after which we ask again.
