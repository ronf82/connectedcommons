12 quick questions

Pick the answer that best describes your company today. Go with your first instinct.

01

Market

Who you sell to and what you say to them. Without a clear buyer, outreach stays broad and nobody feels it was written for them.

1.How clearly can you describe your ideal customer?
2.Do you know the problem your customers had in common when they bought?
3.How do your website and outreach describe who you are for?
02

People

Who owns new business and how much time they have for it. When sales sits with the leadership team, the pipeline goes quiet whenever they get busy.

4.Who works on new business each week?
5.Where do most new deals come from?
6.Do you know when you would add sales headcount?
03

Process

Your pipeline, CRM and follow-up, and whether you know your numbers. A simple, shared process is what makes results repeatable.

7.Where do your leads and deals live?
8.How fast do new leads get a personal reply?
9.How well do you know your sales numbers?
04

AI & tools

How much of the research, list building, drafting and reporting is done by tools, with a person checking the output.

10.How do you research accounts and build lists?
11.How do you write outreach messages?
12.How do you get your weekly pipeline report?
