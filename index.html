About

Who We Are

Connected Commons is a Singapore-based business solutions consultancy. We work with foreign SMEs and funded startups to diagnose challenges, develop tailored solutions, and manage operational growth across Southeast Asia.

Led by an experienced founder and supported by a network of specialist partners, we deliver integrated operations and corporate tooling—tested before implementation. Instead of adding subscription complexity, we provide what works, at a fraction of the cost.
