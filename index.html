About

Who We Are

Connected Commons is a Singapore-based business solutions consultancy. We work with foreign SMEs and funded startups to diagnose challenges, develop tailored solutions, and manage operational growth across Southeast Asia.

Led by an experienced founder and supported by a dedicated team of specialists, we deliver integrated operations and corporate tooling—tested before implementation. Instead of adding subscription complexity, we provide what works, at a fraction of the cost.

Company Credibility

Our collective expertise spans 16+ years across banking, payments, real estate, logistics, and tech/talent outsourcing with a Singapore head office and SEA delivery. Bilingual in English and Chinese. Compliance-grade local AI solutions crafted and available for clients.
