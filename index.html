How We Work

People, process and AI, built around your pipeline

People · The top

Judgement: positioning calls, buyer conversations and partner introductions, led by Ron with our network of specialist partners.

Process · The middle

A GTM playbook: ideal customer profile, messaging, outreach sequences, CRM stages and clear ownership, so your team and the tools work from the same plan.

AI · The foundation

Carries the volume: market and account research, list building, first drafts of outreach and pipeline reporting. A person reviews everything before it goes out.

The shadow · Your problem

A quiet pipeline is usually a symptom. We find the root cause, whether it is the buyer, the message or the follow-up, and fix that first.

How we work, case study

A working product and a quiet pipeline

Illustrative scenario · not a client case study
The situation

A Singapore B2B software company has a dozen paying customers, all from the leadership team's own networks. After a new funding round, the board wants to know where the next fifty will come from.

Step 01

Diagnose

Interview existing customers to find the problem they have in common and the role that signs off on the purchase.

Step 02

Build

Narrow the focus to two industries, rewrite the messaging around that problem, and build a list of named target accounts.

Step 03

Run

Run LinkedIn and email outreach, qualify the replies, and book demos for the leadership team with the buyers who fit.

What the team would have at the end

A tested way to find and qualify buyers, messaging that has been tried in market, and a clear view of which segment to back before adding sales headcount.

Who this is for, and who it isn't

Good fit
