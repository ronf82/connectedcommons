Background

Twenty years of building pipelines in Southeast Asia

I have spent more than 20 years in business development and operations in Southeast Asia, mostly in roles centred on building a business from the ground up. Each sector taught me something I now use with B2B tech teams.

  1. Banking and finance

    Housing mortgages and commercial lending: selling a considered, high-value product to buyers who compare carefully.

  2. Real estate

    Advising property buyers and sellers, where trust and follow-through decide the deal.

  3. Logistics

    How businesses move physical goods across Southeast Asian borders, and what slows them down.

  4. Software and outsourcing

    Business development and operations for software development and outsourcing teams, run from Singapore with delivery across the region, including Indonesia and the Philippines.

Across all of them, one thing mattered most: building relationships and trust with the people you serve. That is the same thing a quiet B2B pipeline usually lacks.

What I work on now

Go-to-market and business development for B2B tech

Through Connected Commons, supported by a network of specialist partners, I help B2B software and tech companies in Singapore and Southeast Asia get from a working product to a working pipeline. AI handles research and first drafts; I make the judgement calls, and a person reviews everything before it goes out.

GTM Strategy

Who to sell to, what to say and which channels to use, in a written plan.

Pipeline Build

Target-account lists, LinkedIn and email outreach, CRM setup and qualification.

Fractional BD Support

Part-time business development for teams not ready for a full-time sales lead.
