About Connected Commons

What does Connected Commons do?

Connected Commons is a boutique go-to-market (GTM) and business development consultancy based in Singapore. We help B2B software and tech companies that already have a working product find, reach and qualify the right buyers, through three services: GTM Strategy, Pipeline Build and Fractional BD Support. We are supported by a network of specialist partners.

Who is behind Connected Commons?

Connected Commons is led by Ron Foo, based in Singapore. Ron has more than 20 years in business development and operations across banking, real estate, logistics, software and outsourcing in Southeast Asia. Connected Commons is a brand operated by RFtechstuff (UEN 53536160B). You can read more on Ron Foo's profile.

Who do you work with?

B2B software and tech companies with a working product and some paying customers, where sales still depends on the leadership team's time and networks. Most are based in, or selling into, Singapore and Southeast Asia, and want a plan plus hands-on help rather than only a report.

Who are you not a good fit for?

Consumer (B2C) businesses, ideas that do not yet have a working product, anyone looking for guaranteed lead numbers or pay-per-lead deals, and companies that want a large outsourced sales floor.

Do you only work with companies in Singapore?

No. We work with companies based in Singapore and with companies elsewhere that are selling into Singapore and Southeast Asia. Work can be done remotely or in person, in English or Chinese.

Services

What is included in GTM Strategy?

GTM Strategy decides who to sell to and what to say before you spend more on sales and marketing. It covers your ideal customer profile and buyer roles, positioning and core messaging, channel priorities and a written GTM plan. It usually takes 3 to 6 weeks.

What is included in Pipeline Build?

Pipeline Build turns the plan into a working pipeline and runs the first campaigns with you. It covers named target-account and contact lists, outreach sequences for LinkedIn and email, CRM setup and qualification criteria. It usually takes 6 to 12 weeks.

What is Fractional BD Support?

Fractional BD Support is part-time business development for teams that are not ready for a full-time sales lead. It covers ongoing outreach and follow-up, partner and channel conversations, and pipeline reviews and reporting to leadership. It runs monthly.

Is fractional business development a replacement for a sales team?

Not permanently. It suits the stage before a sales team makes sense: it lets you test your message and target market in the field first, so any later decision about sales headcount rests on what has actually worked.

Can I start with just one service?

Yes. The three services build on each other (plan, build, run), but you can start at any stage or combine them. We agree the starting point on a call.

How we work

How do you use AI in the work?

AI carries the volume: market and account research, list building, first drafts of outreach and pipeline reporting. People make the judgement calls on positioning, buyer conversations and partner introductions, and a person reviews everything before it goes out.

What happens on the first call?

It is a short call at no cost. We talk through your product, your current customers and where sales is stuck, then work out together whether an engagement makes sense and what it would cover. If we are not the right fit, we will say so.

How long does an engagement take?

GTM Strategy usually takes 3 to 6 weeks and Pipeline Build usually takes 6 to 12 weeks. Fractional BD Support is monthly and ongoing. The exact length depends on your market and team, and is agreed before we start.

Is there a way to check our readiness before talking?

Yes. The free GTM Readiness Scorecard is 12 questions and takes about 3 minutes. It shows your score, your weakest area and your top 3 gaps, with a next step for each.

Pricing and commitments

How much does it cost?

We do not publish a price list, because the work depends on your market, team and starting point. Each engagement is scoped on a call, and you see the scope and fee in writing before any work begins.

Do you guarantee leads or results?

No. We do not sell guaranteed lead numbers or pay-per-lead arrangements. We stand behind our advice and the work we agree in writing, but sales results also depend on your product, market and team.

Practical questions

Is what I share with you confidential?

Yes. What you share stays between us. We only share information with a specialist partner where it is needed to deliver the work, and only with your agreement.

Do you give legal, tax or accounting advice?

No. Connected Commons provides business consulting only. For legal, tax, accounting or financial matters, please speak to a licensed professional.
