1. Who we are

This website, connectedcommons.co, is operated by [OPERATOR NAME, e.g. Ron Foo trading as Connected Commons / Connected Commons Pte. Ltd.] ("Connected Commons", "we", "us"), based in Singapore. We provide business solutions and market-entry support for foreign SMEs and funded startups operating in Southeast Asia.

We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy and handle it in accordance with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (the "PDPA"). Our Data Protection Officer contact is in section 13.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, company name and role, when you fill in a form, join our email list, book a call, message us on WhatsApp or email us.
  • Enquiry details: what you tell us about your business, your expansion plans and your needs, including anything you type into our website chatbot.
  • Engagement information: if you become a client, the information needed to deliver the work, such as company documents, director and shareholder details for incorporation, and invoicing details. This is covered by your engagement agreement as well as this policy.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and referring page, collected by our hosting provider and, if you accept analytics cookies, by our analytics tool.
  • Your cookie preferences, stored on your device. See our Cookie Policy.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive categories of data through this website. Please do not send us government ID numbers, bank details or passwords through forms, the chatbot or WhatsApp. If we need identity documents for an engagement, we will tell you how to send them securely.

7. How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards such as access controls, two-step verification on our business accounts and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No online system is completely secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you significant harm, we will notify you and the Personal Data Protection Commission as the PDPA requires.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may:

  • ask for access to the personal data we hold about you and how it has been used or disclosed in the past year;
  • ask us to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • withdraw your consent to our collection, use or disclosure of your data. Tell us by email and we will explain what this means for the services we can provide, then stop within a reasonable time (usually 10 business days).

To make a request, email hello@connectedcommons.co. We may need to verify your identity first. We aim to respond within 30 days. In some cases the law lets us charge a reasonable fee for access requests or decline a request, and we will explain why if so.
